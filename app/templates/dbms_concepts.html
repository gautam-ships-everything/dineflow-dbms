ACID Properties in Practice: Multi-Table Checkout Transaction Atomic Commit / Rollback

A restaurant checkout involves mutating multiple tables simultaneously: (1) verifying order, (2) inserting payment record into payments, (3) deducting raw ingredient stock from inventory_items via menu_recipes, (4) setting order status to 'Completed', (5) releasing table to 'Available', and (6) crediting customer loyalty points. Under DBMS Atomicity and Consistency, either ALL of these steps persist, or if any error occurs (network failure, stock constraint, gateway timeout), all modifications are completely rolled back.
